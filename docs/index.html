Macro & Fiscal Monitor

Growth, inflation, public finances, debt by sector, housing, foreign trade and exchange rates for selected economies, refreshed automatically from IMF, OECD, BIS, Eurostat, ECB and UN Comtrade data.

A personal project by Elio Bolliger. Data: IMF, OECD, BIS, Eurostat, ECB, UN Comtrade, retrieved automatically through their public APIs. The selection and presentation of data are my own and do not reflect the views of any institution I am affiliated with. Code on GitHub.
